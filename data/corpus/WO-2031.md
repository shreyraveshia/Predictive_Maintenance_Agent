# WO-2031 Past work order: Pump bearing wear
Asset: P-101 | Date: 2025-03-14 | Status: closed

## Symptoms
Vibration rose from 2.1 to 6.9 mm/s over about two weeks, with bearing housing temperature up 4 C.

## Root cause
Worn drive-end and non-drive-end bearings due to degraded grease.

## Action taken
Isolated pump, replaced both bearings, rechecked alignment, regreased. Parts used: BRG-6310-P x2. Downtime: 6 hours.

## Outcome
Vibration returned to 2.0 mm/s and has stayed stable.
