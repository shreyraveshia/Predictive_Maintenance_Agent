# WO-2052 Past work order: Pump seal leakage with pressure loss
Asset: P-106 | Date: 2025-08-20 | Status: closed

## Symptoms
Discharge pressure fell from 6.0 to 4.6 bar over two weeks with a small leak at the gland.

## Root cause
Worn mechanical seal faces.

## Action taken
Replaced the mechanical seal kit, inspected shaft sleeve, flushed seal line. Parts used: SEAL-MS-50 x1. Downtime: 5 hours.

## Outcome
Pressure restored to 6.0 bar; leak stopped.
