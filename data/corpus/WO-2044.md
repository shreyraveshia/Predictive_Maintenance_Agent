# WO-2044 Past work order: Compressor overheating
Asset: C-301 | Date: 2025-06-02 | Status: closed

## Symptoms
Casing temperature rose from 70 C to 88 C over ten days; motor current up about 8 percent.

## Root cause
Intercooler filter blocked with debris, reducing coolant flow.

## Action taken
Cleaned the intercooler and replaced the cooler filter. Parts used: FLT-COOL-C3 x1. Downtime: 4 hours.

## Outcome
Temperature back to 70 C within one hour of restart.
