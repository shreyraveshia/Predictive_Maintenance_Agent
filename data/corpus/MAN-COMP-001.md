# MAN-COMP-001 Air Compressor Maintenance Manual
Applies to: compressor

## 5.1 Overheating from a blocked cooler
Symptoms: discharge and casing temperature ramps from the normal 70 C towards 90 C over one to two weeks, the cooler temperature difference shrinks, and motor current rises by 5-10 percent. Causes: fouled intercooler, clogged cooler filter, or low coolant flow. Warning level is 80 C; shut down at 90 C. Action: clean the cooler, replace the cooler filter (FLT-COOL-C3), and check coolant flow and level.

## 5.2 Low discharge pressure
Symptoms: slow decline in delivery pressure below its normal 6.5 bar; below 5.0 bar is degraded and below 4.5 bar is critical. Causes: leaking valve reeds, worn seals, or a clogged intake filter. Action: inspect valves and replace the reed set (VLV-REED-C3), then check the intake filter and seals.

## 5.3 Compressor bearing vibration
Rising vibration on a compressor is diagnosed with the severity and fault-pattern guidance in MAN-VIB-001. Confirm with a bearing inspection before replacement.
