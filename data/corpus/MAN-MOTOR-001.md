# MAN-MOTOR-001 Electric Motor Maintenance Manual
Applies to: motor

## 3.1 Bearing wear
Symptoms: rising vibration over days to weeks together with a frame temperature rise of 3-6 C and increasing bearing noise. Vibration above 4.5 mm/s warrants inspection; above 7.1 mm/s requires shutdown planning. Action: isolate per MAN-SPARES-001 section 2, replace both bearings (BRG-6312-M), check coupling alignment, and regrease. Typical downtime: 8 hours.

## 3.2 Overheating from restricted cooling
Symptoms: frame temperature climbs steadily above the normal operating range of 60-65 C. Warning level is 80 C; the trip level is 90 C. Causes: blocked cooling fins or fan cover, failed cooling fan, or poor ventilation. Motor current may rise a few percent as temperature increases. Action: clean fins and fan cover, inspect the cooling fan (FAN-MOT-200), and verify ventilation. Do not run the motor above 90 C.

## 3.3 Insulation resistance testing
Routine check every 12 months: measure insulation resistance between the electrical circuit and the frame with the motor isolated. Record the value and compare with the previous reading. This is a scheduled inspection, not a fault diagnosis procedure.
