# WO-2060 Past work order: Motor bearing wear
Asset: M-201 | Date: 2025-09-11 | Status: closed

## Symptoms
Vibration rose from 1.8 to 6.2 mm/s and frame temperature rose 5 C over twelve days.

## Root cause
Bearing wear with slight coupling misalignment.

## Action taken
Replaced both bearings and realigned the coupling. Parts used: BRG-6312-M x2. Downtime: 8 hours.

## Outcome
Vibration back to 1.8 mm/s.
