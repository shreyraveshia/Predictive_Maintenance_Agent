# MAN-SPARES-001 Spare Parts Catalogue, Safe Isolation and Priority Guide
Applies to: pump, motor, compressor

## 1 Parts catalogue
BRG-6310-P: bearing set for centrifugal pumps. BRG-6312-M: bearing set for electric motors. SEAL-MS-50: mechanical seal kit for pumps. FLT-COOL-C3: cooler filter for compressors. VLV-REED-C3: valve reed set for compressors. FAN-MOT-200: cooling fan for motors.

## 2 Safe isolation (lockout-tagout)
Step 1: notify operations and obtain a permit to work. Step 2: stop the machine using normal controls. Step 3: isolate electrical supply, then lock and tag the isolator. Step 4: depressurise and drain the machine. Step 5: verify zero energy by attempting a restart and testing for absence of voltage. Step 6: only then begin work.

## 3 Work order priority guidance
P1: confirmed fault on a criticality-3 asset, or any confirmed fault with imminent failure risk. P2: confirmed fault on other assets, or predicted high risk on a criticality-3 asset. P3: predicted risk at medium level. P4: routine or low-risk monitoring. A predicted risk must never be described as a confirmed fault.
