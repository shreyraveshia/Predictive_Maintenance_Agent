# MAN-PUMP-001 Centrifugal Pump Maintenance Manual
Applies to: pump

## 4.1 Bearing wear
Symptoms: a steady rise in overall vibration velocity (mm/s) over days to weeks, usually with a small rise in bearing housing temperature of 2-6 C. Causes: lubricant degradation, contamination, misalignment, or normal fatigue. Vibration above 4.5 mm/s warrants inspection; above 7.1 mm/s continued operation risks bearing seizure. Action: isolate the pump per MAN-SPARES-001 section 2, replace both bearings (BRG-6310-P), check shaft alignment, and regrease with approved lubricant. Typical downtime: 6 hours.

## 4.2 Mechanical seal leakage
Symptoms: a slow decline in discharge pressure (about 0.05-0.15 bar per day) with possible visible leakage at the gland; vibration stays unchanged. Discharge pressure below 5.0 bar indicates degraded performance; below 4.5 bar the duty point is no longer met. Action: replace the seal kit (SEAL-MS-50), inspect the shaft sleeve for scoring, and verify the seal flush line is clear.

## 4.3 Cavitation
Symptoms: erratic, noisy vibration with a crackling sound, fluctuating discharge pressure and low suction pressure. This is not a gradual trend; it appears and disappears with flow conditions. Causes: blocked suction strainer or insufficient suction head. Action: clear the strainer, check the suction valve is fully open, and reduce throttling.
